## Design Context

### Users
People evaluating Rodrigo's work for collaboration, freelance opportunities, or developer roles. They need to understand the portfolio quickly and have an obvious, low-friction way to start a conversation.

### Brand Personality
Technical, expressive, and confident. The experience should feel personal and memorable without sacrificing clarity or professionalism.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark retro-futurist / cyberpunk direction with pixel display typography, a restrained neon pink-orange accent, and strong editorial composition. Avoid repetitive card grids, generic glassmorphism, and visual noise.

### Design Principles
- Make one primary action unmistakable.
- Use asymmetry and spacing to create hierarchy instead of repeated containers.
- Keep the cyberpunk character through typography and accent details, not constant glow effects.
- Prefer personal, direct copy over generic portfolio language.
- Keep interactive elements keyboard-accessible and readable on small screens.
